From Personal AI Routines to Shared Work

Human Agency · Knowledge · Organisational Continuity

The next question became organisational: when personally useful AI routines enter a shared workflow, who understands them, who reviews their outputs, and can someone else continue the work when a person or tool changes?

Read the independent research concept →

Central research question

Under what conditions do personalised AI routines become useful shared practices—and when do they create dependencies that make work harder to understand, question or continue?

Analytical lens

Personal augmentation becomes interesting when it meets interdependence.

01

Agency

Can people understand, question, revise and influence AI-supported work?

02

Dependency

Who becomes necessary for explanation, checking, access or continuation?

03

Continuity

Can useful work continue when a person, tool or configuration changes?

Proposed study

Follow the workflow, not just the individual.

04Walkthroughs
